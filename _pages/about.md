I am Tomasz Kowalski, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Baltic Computing University. I work in the Dependable Software Systems Group, which is led by Professor Leila Haddad, who supervises my doctoral research. I graduated from Krakow Polytechnic in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interest in distributed system reliability, program repair, and production failure diagnosis.
